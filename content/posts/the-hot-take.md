---
title: "Master languages before frameworks"
summary: "Committing to a stack is too limiting."
kind: take
verdict: Scorching
date: 2025-11-25
---

Committing to a stack is too limiting. If you're going to get into the tech world, **master programming languages and data management before frameworks**. There's always going to be something new, but it's often just an older language under the hood.
